My story

I was the shyest girl in the entire class. Speaking in front of people felt impossible. But something inside refused to stay small. I started with one sentence, then one speech, then one debate.

Today? I founded "Indigo Skies" Debate Club — now home to over 200 members. From silence to leading a community of voices. That transformation taught me something powerful: education isn't just about books — it's about unlocking who you can become.

If you want to destroy a country, destroy its education system.
— Because education is the foundation of everything.

Now I teach English, build startups, and help students find internships. Because I believe every young person deserves a mentor who sees their potential before they see it themselves.
